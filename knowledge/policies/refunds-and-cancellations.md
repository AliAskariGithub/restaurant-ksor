If a dine-in order is incorrect or below quality standards, staff will replace it free of charge or offer a comparable credit, decided on the spot.

For takeaway or delivery orders, refunds are issued to the original payment method within **3–5 business days** if the order was incorrect, incomplete, or arrived spoiled. Report issues within 24 hours of receiving the order.

Once a meal has been fully consumed, no refund is issued except for a genuine food-safety concern, which is reviewed case by case.

For reservation cancellation terms, see the [reservation policy](reservations.md).
