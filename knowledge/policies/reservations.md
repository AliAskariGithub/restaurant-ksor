Reservations can be made by phone up to 14 days in advance. A table is held for **15 minutes** past the booked time before it may be released to walk-ins.

Parties of **8 or more** require a deposit of Rs. 2000, deducted from the final bill. The deposit is forfeited if the reservation is cancelled with less than **3 hours' notice**.

Reservations for smaller parties can be cancelled any time before arrival with no penalty.
