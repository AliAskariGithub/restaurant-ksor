**Do you have vegan options?**
Yes — Vegetable Samosas, Vegetable Biryani, Roti, and Mint Lemonade are vegan. Daal Chawal can be made vegan on request (ghee omitted).

**Is delivery available?**
Takeaway is available. See the [refund and cancellation policy](policies/refunds-and-cancellations.md) for delivery order issues.

**Do you accommodate large groups?**
Yes — see the [reservation policy](policies/reservations.md) for deposit terms on parties of 8 or more.

**Do you accept cards?**
Yes, both cards and cash are accepted.
