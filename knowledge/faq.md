**Do you have vegan options?**
Yes — Vegetable Samosas, Vegetable Biryani, Roti, and Mint Lemonade are vegan. Daal Chawal can be made vegan on request (ghee omitted).

**Is delivery available?**
Yes, delivery and takeaway are both available. See the [refund and cancellation policy](policies/refunds-and-cancellations.md) for delivery order issues.

**Do you accommodate large groups?**
Yes — see the [reservation policy](policies/reservations.md) for deposit terms on parties of 8 or more.

**Do you accept cards?**
Yes, both cards and cash are accepted.

**Do you provide nutritional information or calorie counts?**
No — we list ingredients and allergens for every dish, but we do not track or publish calorie or macronutrient information.

**Can I see calories for a specific dish, like the Chicken Biryani?**
We don't provide calorie counts for any menu item. See the allergen guide for ingredients and dietary tags instead.

**Do you offer catering for weddings or events?**
We are a dine-in restaurant only. We do not provide catering services.

**Can I bring my own cake for a birthday celebration?**
Outside food is not permitted.
